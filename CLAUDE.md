# Nightclub Nova

The main project is **Club Nova**, an isometric nightclub tycoon game in `game/` (Phaser 3 + Vite). The repo root also has two Python art generators (`generate.py` for posters, `bar.py` for an isometric bar sprite). See README.md for the layout.

See ROADMAP.md for the plan. The reference for all art, UI and gameplay is Nightclub City; art/REFERENCE_NOTES.md lists what we take from the owner's screenshots. Match its style with original assets; never copy its images, logo or name.

## Working on the game

- Run everything from `game/`: `npm install`, `npm run dev`, `npm test`.
- `npm test` builds `dist/index.html` and runs `tests/smoke.mjs` in headless Chromium. Run it before every commit, and add a check to it when adding a feature.
- The build is a single self-contained HTML file (vite-plugin-singlefile, all assets inlined). It must keep working when opened from disk via `file://`.
- `ClubScene` is split across `src/scene/*.js` as mixin classes, merged by `applyMixins()`. Put a new method in the file that matches its topic; `applyMixins` throws if two files define the same name.
- New buyable items go in `src/catalog.js`. Sprites go in `src/assets/sprites/` and are listed in `src/assets.js`.
- Flat surfaces (dance floors, and later wallpaper) are drawn in code, not Blender: designs live in `src/floors.js` / `src/walls.js` and are added to the catalog with `floorTier()` / `wallTier()`. Painted wall sections are saved under `wallpaper` in the save. Regular floors (`FLOOR_PAINTS`, `paintTier()`) are painted tile by tile like wallpaper (`src/scene/floorPaint.js`, saved under `floorPaint`); dance floors (`floorTier()`) are placed props, and the only tiles patrons dance on. A `flow` design needs `frames` to be a multiple of `period`.
- Sprite calibration (`displayWidth`, `originX`/`originY`) was measured against the actual rendered PNGs. Re-measure it if a sprite is re-rendered.
- The bare room is beaten-up torn wallpaper (`tornPaper` in walls.js, three variations picked per section, drawn under any wallpaper by `drawBareWalls()`; Old Brick and Brick are level 5 wallpapers) and one seamless worn-concrete floor (`bareFloorCanvas()` in floors.js, `drawBareFloor()`). Floor tiles are invisible polygons, only tinted while highlighted. A new club (no save) gets `placeStarterLayout()`: the booth on the left wall with a 3x3 Basic Floor (the level 1 dance floor; Checker is level 4) in front of it, and a long bar of four Starter Bar units with one bartender on the right wall. Bar units side by side, facing the same way, join into one long bar worked by one bartender (`barGroup()` / `isWorked()` in staff.js); check `isWorked(rec)`, not `rec.staff`, for whether a bar sells drinks. How many bartenders you may hire depends on level (`BARTENDERS` in config.js: 1, then +1 at levels 4, 7, 11, 15; `bartenderAllowance()`); extras can join a long bar already worked (up to one per unit), spread along it by `staffBarGroup()`.
- The street outside (road, sidewalk, buildings, velvet-rope line, bouncer, passers-by) is `src/scene/street.js`, settings in `STREET` in config.js. The door stays near the back of the left wall (`doorTile()` in world.js). The doorway (`doorZone()`: tiles within 2 steps of the door) is kept clear: it's part of `keepOffTiles()` (with the bar lines and the tiles at each end of a bar), which every spot picker skips; spot pickers also prefer the least crowded options (`crowdAt()` / `leastCrowded()` in activities.js) so guests spread out; a guest who comes in first walks to a clear spot just inside (`entrySpot()`, `patron.entering`) before choosing an activity, and leavers always walk all the way out. Like Nightclub City, the line stands on the sidewalk outside the left wall, from the door toward the front, on a red carpet behind a rope; it's drawn on `streetBackLayer`, under the walls, `STREET.lineOut` tiles out so the wall hides about the bottom half of each person (never draw street people over the walls). People in line face the door (`faceDoor()`, which re-plays the idle clip so the back-facing pose shows). Passers-by walk all four sidewalks; the left and back ones are on `streetBackLayer` too. In headless Chromium the game's timers crawl (slow frames, capped delta), so tests call street and timer methods directly instead of waiting.
- A DJ booth's footprint includes the DJ's own two tiles behind the desk (`backTiles` in catalog.js, tagged `.back` by `getFootprint()`); art and draw order use `deskTiles()`. A booth saved against the wall is moved out a tile on load.
- The club always has exactly one DJ booth with a permanent DJ (`ensureClubBooth()` / `upgradeClubBooth()` in `staff.js`); only bartenders are hired. Music is `src/music.js`. The Bass Boost and Drink Rush buttons are `src/scene/boost.js` (`BOOST`, `RUSH`): each shows a big popup (`showBigPopup()` in hud.js), Bass Boost flashes the screen and pulses light across the dance floors (`pulseDanceFloor()`), Drink Rush lights up the worked bars (`highlightBars()`); each raises a weight in `activityWeights()` for a while, and `rallyGuests()` has `joinShare` of the guests who can (`canRally()`: not leaving, arguing, in a bar line or already doing it) react at once and set off a moment later (`patron.wantActivity`, taken first by `chooseActivity()`). When a boost starts, everyone on the dance floor goes wild for `BOOST.exciteMs` (`exciteDancer()`: much faster dancing, heart eyes or ! popping up); the rest of the boost dancers dance a little faster (`energize()`, applied in `setPatronAnimation()`) and longer. Quick reactions over guests' heads (happy face, excited face, drink, note) are `popReaction()` in `src/scene/reactions.js` (`REACTIONS` in config.js), drawn as textures in code.
- The club runs one endless night like Nightclub City (no night clock or summary): `src/scene/rating.js` keeps `doorsOpen()` / `clubOpen()` always true, and every `RATING.sampleMs` rates the crowd's average vibe in stars (+ bonus fans); `clubRating()` averages the last few (saved under `nightStars`). Income goes through `noteIncome()` (running totals).
- Parties are `src/scene/parties.js`, the list in `PARTIES` in config.js. Their effects go through `partyEffect(name, none)` (capacity, arrivals, tips, thirst, fans); one at a time. Throwing one starts a `PARTY_COUNTDOWN_MS` countdown (`partyPhase` 'countdown', then 'running' for `PARTY_LENGTH_MS`; `currentParty()` is only the running one), shown on the banner. A party's job is to fill the club and boost tips, dancing and drinking (no celebrities). When it starts, its `crowd` joins the line outside via `callPartyCrowd()`, and the bouncer lets them in one every `STREET.partyAdmitMs`. When it ends, party guests leave over `leaveOverMs`, the party pays fans (`XP.partyPerGuest`, capped at `XP.partyMax`), and a wrap-up popup (`showPartySummary()`, `#partySummary`) lists what happened while it ran: `partyStats` (guests, celebrities, drinks, drink revenue, bonuses, average happiness from `samplePartyMood()`, fights / ejections) exists only while a party runs.
- The dock along the bottom is the game's navigation (`src/scene/shop.js`): one glass bar (`#navBar`) of labelled pads (`.dockTab.navPad`, `data-nav`): Build, Staff, Club, Inventory and Goals, the open one glowing in its colour, and the green OK (`#storeOk`, shown while a panel is open) at its end; the open panel sits above the bar. Each panel (`setDockTab()`: 'decor', 'staff', 'edit', 'expand', 'celebs', 'inventory', 'goals') belongs to one pad (`NAV_OF`; `activeDockKey()` gives the lit pad). Build ('decor') and Club have a row of keys on the panel's top edge (`data-mode="store"`, keys tagged with their `data-nav`): Club's are Edit, Expand and Celebrities (`CLUB_KEYS`; the Club pad reopens the last one, `lastClubTab`). Goals shows the three goals in place of the item row. Icons are clean white glyphs in `ICONS` in `src/uiIcons.js` (keys give the colour); the dock pads, the cash and Bass Boost are detailed full-colour drawings in `ART` (`artShop`, `catStaff`, `artClub`, `artStorage`, `artGoals`, `artCeleb`, `artSpeaker`, `cash`), as are a few others (give each its own gradient ids, since browsers skip gradients defined inside hidden elements). The dock starts closed (`data-mode="closed"`: just the bar); clicking a pad opens its panel, clicking it again closes it, and OK finishes the current action and closes it (`closeDock()`). Build's keys: NEW first (`isNewItem()`: unlocked at the last two levels; those items carry a NEW tag everywhere), then the store categories (floors and dance floors share Floors and take turns unlocking, one a level). Edit's tools are Move, Turn, Put away, Sell and Clear Club (`EDIT_TOOLS`; Clear Club, `clearClub()`, asks first in `#clearConfirm` and puts everything but the DJ booth and bars into storage). The inventory and the Edit tools (move, turn, put away, sell) are `src/scene/inventory.js`; they work on floor tiles too (dance floor tiles are placed props; a painted floor tile goes through `editFloorTile()`: move picks it up to lay again free from the inventory, sell refunds half and takes its XP back, and the bare floor shows underneath); the inventory is saved under `inventory`, and things placed from it are free. The DJ booth can't be put away or sold, but Move picks it up with its DJ (`movingBooth`); calling the move off puts it back, and a save mid-move keeps it where it was. Things in the inventory place whatever their unlock level. With something in hand, a click places it, never opens a guest's card. While something is held, `updateGhost()` always shows it under the pointer, see-through, with the tiles it would take filled and outlined (`footprintFill` on the floor, `footprintOutline` on top): green where it fits, red where it doesn't (the picture tinted red too). What you're holding is named in `#selectedChip` at the top of the screen, clear of the floor. Cards that refresh often (Staff, Expand) only rebuild when their content changes.
- The room is `gridW` tiles along gx (the right wall) by `gridH` along gy (the left wall), not always square; check bounds with `inGrid(gx, gy)`. Expanding adds one row at a time along the front-left (`gridH`+1) or front-right (`gridW`+1) edge (`expandClub(side)` in world.js, prices and per-level wall limits in `EXPANSION` in catalog.js). The Expand tab shows the new row in green (`showExpandPreview()`) when a side card is hovered or picked, and a green Build it! card confirms. Saved as `gridW`/`gridH`; older saves' square `gridSize` still loads.
- Capacity is `patronCapacity()` in economy.js: `CAPACITY.base` (8) plus `CAPACITY.perExpansion` per row of floor bought (counted from `gridW`/`gridH` past `BASE_GRID_SIZE`). Staff don't count, celebrities do (`guestCount()`); when full, arrivals wait in the line outside. The HUD shows it as "n/max" over a Guests label. Dance floors and parties don't add capacity.
- Levelling up opens a menu of everything that level unlocks, with pictures (`showLevelUp()` / `unlocksAt()` in hud.js); tests stub it out in `waitForScene()` so it doesn't cover the buttons. `iconSvg()` gives each copy of a drawing its own gradient ids.
- HUD buttons and shop keys are drawn icons (`src/uiIcons.js`) with no words on them (only the dock's pads have a name label under the picture); their name and description go in `data-tip-name` / `data-tip-text`, shown by the shared hover tip (`src/tooltips.js`). Put live state (timers, "club closed") in the tip text, not on the button.
- The HUD (index.html, style.css) is "neon candy", kept quiet so the eye goes to the club first: a few dark purple glass panels (`--glass`, `--glass-shadow`), dark screens (`--screen`) for dialogs and the shop items, and glossy candy buttons with a white rim and a shine, all from one shared rule that takes the colour from `--c` (round buttons, dialog buttons, song controls); hovering one makes it bounce (`boing`). Prices are gold pills, NEW/VIP tags tilted stickers, titles gold with a pink glow, a bold display font (`--display-font`); money and numbers use the clean `--money-font`, written like "$3,000" (`formatMoney()` in util.js). The stat meters bounce on the music's beat: `beatUI(kind)` in hud.js, called with every kick and clap from speakers.js, sets `body.kick` / `body.clap` and each stat box's `--meter`. Top left is the player panel (`#hudProfile`): the record with the level badge, the XP bar, the cash under it (it rolls up to a new amount, `rollCash()`, `.rolling` while it moves), tiny How to play / New club buttons, and a smaller song row with like, skip and sound (`#songBox`; how long the song has left is in its hover tip). Top right (`#hudRight`): the stats (rating, luxury, guests) in one panel and the Bass Boost / Drink Rush / Party buttons in one row under it, the same width. The right edge has the drink meter (`src/scene/meter.js`, `METER` in config.js): a glass cylinder of purple liquid (`--fill`) filled by drinks, tips and bonuses through `noteIncome()` (`meterIncome()`); full, it doubles drink prices for `METER.doubleMs` (`drinkPriceFactor()` in `serveDrink()`) while it drains, then empties. There are no zoom buttons: the wheel and + / - zoom. Every dialog is a `.summaryCard` or `.levelCard` with `.summaryButton` pads. Hover tips stay white speech bubbles. Money over guests pops up with `floatMoney()` in staff.js, always green (the owner asked for no red money); "+1.5x Tip!" rides above it while a boost or party multiplies tips; other floating words use `floatText()`.
- Guests with a drink (`pickDrinkingSpot()`) usually go and sit down with it (`VISIT.drinkSeatChance`, any free seat; `sitDown()` keeps them seated until the drink is done), else stand somewhere quiet near the bar, never on a dance floor tile. Guest visits are `src/scene/activities.js`: a guest stays `VISIT.visitMs` (4-8 min) and moves through activities (drink, dance, sit, chat, wander; durations in `VISIT`), picked by their type's weights (`GUEST_TYPES`); `patron.activity` holds what they're doing, and they leave once the visit is over and they've finished it. `pickRoamTarget()` hands off to `nextActivityStep()`, and arriving anywhere goes through `arriveForActivity()`. Every drink goes through `serveDrink()`, which starts the drinking activity. Bar lines and bartenders are `src/scene/bars.js` (settings `BAR` in config.js): a guest going for a drink (`joinBarQueue()`) takes a free service spot in front of any unit of a long bar (`patron.atSpot`), or waits in rows behind (`barWaitTiles()`) and steps up when one frees (`refreshBarLine()`). Bartenders walk side to side along the bar (`rec.staff.atUnit`, `bartenderWalk()`), each claiming a different spot (`unit.claimedBy`), take `BAR.serveMs` to mix, then serve (`tickBars()`), only to a customer standing still on their spot (`atServiceSpot()`), never one still walking or waiting behind. Bottoms Up! (clicking the bartender) is the exception: it serves the whole line at once, walking and waiting guests included. Nobody gives up before `BAR.patienceMs`; after that some leave with an angry face (`checkBarPatience()`). A guest thirsty for `VISIT.thirstGraceMs` wraps up what they're doing and goes for a drink. Stools are ordinary seats (the owner tried stools as bar service seats and asked for them to be removed). The UI calls it XP everywhere (the code and save still call it `fans`): it comes mainly from service (`XP.drinkServed`), satisfied visits (`LEAVING_FANS`) and parties, plus `XP.perDollar` of every purchase (furniture, decorations, floors, wallpaper, expansions; `awardPurchaseXp()` in economy.js). A placed item keeps the XP it gave (`rec.xp`, saved as `xp`), carried through the inventory (`inventoryXp`, a list per type) when moved or stored, and selling takes it back (`revokePurchaseXp()`), so buying and selling can't farm levels. Levels follow `LEVEL_FANS` (`fansToNextLevel()` / `fansForLevel()`).
- Guests have no lasting thought bubbles over their heads (the owner asked for them to go); only the quick fading reactions.
- Clickable bonuses are `src/scene/bonuses.js` (`BONUS` in config.js; `this.bonuses` holds every badge showing): every 30-60 s a happy guest holds up a high five or fist bump (a gold badge, not a reaction); clicking it within 8 s pays $88. Guests also now and then admire a decoration (the 'admire' activity in activities.js, `ADMIRE` in config.js: `admireSpot()` picks a reachable free tile beside one they haven't admired within `cooldownMs`; after `lookMs` they say WOW!/OUU! and offer a tip coin, `offerBonus(p, 'tip', amount)`). `bonusAt()` / `clickBonus()` run first in the pointer handlers, so a badge click never opens a card or places, moves or sells anything.
- Hovering and clicking go by what's drawn under the cursor, pixel for pixel, nearest the camera first (`objectAt()` in `src/scene/selection.js`, settings `HOVER`): guests, bartenders, the DJ, the guard and furniture. Hovering one gives it an outline glow (Phaser preFX glow on its sprites: every layer of a bar, all of a three-tile booth). With nothing in hand, clicking furniture selects the whole piece (a green glow and its whole footprint outlined on the floor), the Edit tools act on the piece clicked anywhere on it, and a person opens their card (`clickObject()`); right-click sells the piece under the cursor. Bonus badges come first.
- Guests and bartenders are clickable (`clickPerson()` in `src/scene/guests.js`, which also has names, the info card and Bottoms Up!). The card follows Nightclub City's layout: stat bubbles on top, portrait and speech bubble, round action buttons underneath (seat at a VIP booth, send to dance, drink on the house) that grey out with the reason as their hover tip. Only `VIP_BOOTHS` in catalog.js can be chosen for seating a guest. Drinks go through `serveDrink()` in staff.js. Luxury is `luxury()` in economy.js (settings `LUXURY` in config.js).
- Guests getting along or not is `src/scene/security.js` (settings `SECURITY` in config.js): neighbouring dancers sometimes dance together (`maybeDanceTogether()`); a chat now and then becomes an argument (`maybeArgue()`, 💢 icons, `patron.arguing`), the security guard (`this.guard`, posted inside the door, not saved) walks over and usually settles it, otherwise it becomes a dust-cloud fight and one guest is ejected (`patron.ejected`, leaves for no fans). One argument at a time, with a cooldown.
- The Celebrity List is `src/scene/celebrities.js` (the list is `CELEBRITIES` in config.js: key, name, unlock level, fame 1-5, look; `CELEB` for fees, liking, return times, tips and fans). A celebrity's first visit is by paid invitation (`inviteCelebrity()`, fee `CELEB.inviteCost` by fame, from the Celebrities panel; pending invites are saved as `celebInvited`). How good a time they have changes how much they like the club (`celebVisitOver()`, from `patronLeaves()`: their mood, a VIP booth seat (`patron.vipSeated`, set in `sitDown()`), a drink on the house (`patron.onTheHouse`), the club rating; leaving angry costs a lot). Once they've been and like it at least `minLikingToReturn`, they come back on their own, sooner the more they like it (`celebReturnMs()`, `celebDropIn()`, asked by each street arrival); at `regularLiking` they're a VIP regular. What each thinks is saved under `celebs` (`celebState`: { visits, liking }). They queue in their own look with a star (`addStarIcon()`); walking in shows a big "Celebrity Arrival!" popup (`welcomeCelebrity()`) and the crowd reacts (`celebFanfare()`: star eyes or excitement, and some throw tips). `patron.celeb` is their entry; the guest card shows their liking. The Celebrities panel (`renderCelebCards()`) shows each one's portrait, name, stars, a liking bar, a VIP tag for regulars, and their fee or status (`celebStatus()`: locked / available / invited / coming / inside); the level-up menu lists new ones. Parties don't bring celebrities; there are no regular VIP guests any more (old saves' `vips` are ignored).
- Goals are `src/scene/goals.js` (the chain is `GOALS` in config.js: id, text, stat, target, cash, xp). Three show at a time in the dock's Goals panel (`#goals`, `setDockTab('goals')` / `toggleGoals()`; a finished goal makes the Goals pad cheer and puts a ! on it until opened, `seenGoals()`); progress is a counter bumped where things happen (`bumpGoal(stat)`), a best seen while playing (`noteGoalBest()`, checked in `tickGoals()`), or live (level, capacity, rating, in `goalProgress()`). `completeGoal()` pays cash and XP; saved as `goalsDone` / `goalStats`. Tests' `waitForScene()` stubs the payout so cash checks stay exact.
- Songs are `src/scene/songs.js` (the tracks are `TRACKS` in music.js, settings `SONGS`); the club rating is `clubRating()` in rating.js (`RATING`, saved under `nightStars`).
- The save format lives in `src/scene/save.js` under the key `clubNovaSave_v2` (v2 came with the finer grid; v1 saves don't fit it). Don't break existing saves; bump the key only if the format changes incompatibly.

## Art pipeline

- New props are modelled in Blender with a script in `art/blender/`, using `iso_rig.py` for the camera, lighting and rendering. Run it with a Python that has `bpy==4.5.4` and pillow.
- Models are built at the original scale (1 Blender unit = one of the old 64 px tiles); `render_facings()` scales them by `iso_rig.MODEL_SCALE` (4/3) onto the game's finer 48 px tiles, so a model covers about a third more tiles than its size in units, and footprints in the catalog are in game tiles (bars 1x3, DJ booths 2x1 (models scaled by `BOOTH_SCALE` 0.75 in build_booths.py), couches/pool table 3x1, curved booths 3x3). `iso_rig.make_bar_piece()` squeezes a bar module horizontally to fit exactly 1x3 tiles, so bars placed in a row line up into one long bar. Model the prop centred on the origin with its front facing -Y. Game +gx is Blender +X, and game +gy is Blender -Y.
- `render_facings()` renders facings 0/90/180/270 (the model rotated about Z), crops all four to one shared box, and writes `<name>.json` (displayWidth, originX, originY). The catalog imports that JSON, so never hand-tune these numbers.
- Check new art in the game with the footprint tiles outlined before shipping it.
- After rendering new art, run `python3 art/compress_sprites.py` to shrink the PNGs (keeps the build well under the play link's 16 MB).
- Decorations are in `build_decor.py`. `--preview DIR name...` renders facing 0 only, for a quick look before the full render. For scale: a bar counter is 1.0 tall and a patron about 1.5. Avoid `metal=1` materials (they render black with no environment) and faces that sit exactly on top of each other.
- The Spotlight's glow is drawn in the game, not in its render (the emissive lens renders dark): `createSpotBeam()` in lighting.js adds a beam, halo and lens for any catalog item with `spotBeam`, using the lens position per facing measured from its PNGs (`SPOT_LENS`); the lamp tilts up and back, away from its front. Re-measure `SPOT_LENS` if the spotlight is re-rendered. The Disco Ball's glow (halo cycling colours, turning rays, sparkles) is drawn the same way by `createDiscoGlow()` (`discoGlow` in the catalog, ball position in `DISCO`). Speakers (`speakerCones` in the catalog) get drawn cones on top of their rendered ones that bump to the beat (`src/scene/speakers.js`; cone positions worked out from the model in `CONES` with the px-per-unit measured on the speaker tower; beats come from `Music.onStep`, or the song's tempo without sound). `createGlowFx()` / `destroyGlowFx()` add and remove all of these when a prop is placed, turned or removed.
- Every prop render gets dark Freestyle outlines (`iso_rig.add_outlines()`, called by `render_facings()`), like Nightclub City's drawn art. Glowing parts (emission) are left without outlines so neon stays bright.
- Seating is in `build_seating.py`, with the newer booths and sofas (each its own shape) in `seating_designs.py`. Parts named `Back*` (backrests) go behind seated patrons unless, at that facing, they're nearer the camera than the seat closest to them; `Canopy*` parts (roofs, arches overhead) always go behind; everything else is the front layer, which the game always draws on top (`frontAlwaysNear`). Each piece lists its `seats` and `sitLift` in its JSON; a seat may have a third number, the way it faces in degrees from the piece's front (so benches can face each other). Booths are 3x3, sofas 3x1 and the Wood Lounge 3x2 (`LOUNGE_3X2`); every booth is in `VIP_BOOTHS`. Patrons sitting is in `src/scene/seating.js`.
- Bars render in two layers too (`render_facings(..., layers=split_counter(root))`): `<name>_back_*` and `<name>_front_*`. The game draws whichever is nearer the camera on top, with the bartender between them. Any prop staff stand inside should do the same.
- Patrons are drawn art (front view left, back view right, on white): every outfit is the same base guy with different clothes and hair. `art/sprites_from_art.py IMAGE INDEX` cuts him into head, body, arms and legs and animates them into a sheet (idle/walk/dance/sit x front/back; layout, frame counts and fps in `patrons.json`). Sit frames keep the torso where it is standing and fold the legs up under the hips, so seats calibrated for a standing patron still fit; `art/recolor_patron.py` makes extra patrons by changing skin tone and clothes colour. Sheets 0-5 are the drawn outfits, 6-11 recolours. Front faces screen down-left and back faces up-right; the game mirrors both for the other diagonals. (`build_patrons.py` is the old Blender chibi generator, no longer used.)
- Girl patrons will use the owner's girl base model (chibi, T-pose, long hair, T-shirt and shorts), described in `art/characters/README.md`. Her 3D model is `art/characters/girl_base.blend`; `art/blender/paint_girl.py SRC LOOK OUT.png` paints it in one of its `LOOKS` (no UVs, so prints and fabrics are projected from the front) and renders the front. She isn't rigged or animated yet; when she is, follow the guy's sheet layout.
- Guests that move like the owner's own 3D model (`art/characters/character_model_1*`, rigged and animated in Mixamo; clips in `art/mixamo/model1/`: walk, dance, sit, sittalk, drink) but look like the drawn guests: `art/blender/build_patron_hybrid.py`. `render` (Blender, ~1h) renders every frame of the model's body, its own head removed, as a cel-shaded white shade pass with outlines plus a flat id pass (skin / shirt / trousers / shoes / soles / glass), and notes the neck point, head tilt and facing per frame (`art/build/hybrid/`, not committed); `compose N...` (PIL, seconds) gives each a drawn guest's head (cut from their sheet at the chin, 60% of the standing height above the soles; front or back view, mirrored to match the face) and that guest's outfit colours (sampled from their sheet), writing `patron_3d_hybridNN.png/.json`. The body is chunkier than the model (`BUILD` bone scales) and has clothing details marked in the id pass by rest-pose position (`add_details()`: cuffs / collar / hem as trim, waistband, trouser turn-ups, a shoe stripe in the drawn shoes' second colour); the id pass renders with one sample and no denoising so its key colours stay exact. The owner found the fully 3D look (`build_patron_model1.py`, `patron_3d_teal`, and the cel-shaded `concept_model1.py`) odd next to the drawn guests, so only the hybrids are in the game. Each sheet has its own grid in its JSON (frame size, anchor, `starts` per clip_facing, frames and fps per clip); `patronMetaOf(i)` in assets.js gives any character's grid, the drawn ones from patrons.json. They come after the 12 drawn sheets (`MODEL_PATRONS`) and make up `MODEL_PATRON_SHARE` of new guests. They have two more clips: `drink` (a pink glass in the left hand, played while drinking) and `sittalk` (half of seated guests, `SIT_TALK_CHANCE` in seating.js); drawn guests fall back to idle and sit (`CLIP_FALLBACK` in patrons.js). The sit clips are lifted so the hips are at standing height, like the drawn guests' sit frames, so seat calibration fits both. There's no standing idle clip yet: idle holds the drink's first frame until `idle.fbx` is added.
- Shelved 3D patron experiment: the owner's Mixamo character (`art/mixamo/walk.fbx`, a blank untextured model) and `art/blender/build_patron3d.py`, which colours him, adds hair and a painted face, pins the walk in place and renders `patron_12.png` in the drawn sheets' frame grid. The owner judged it not good enough, so it is not in the game. Bringing it back needs that sheet plus game code (see commit 990efdd).

## Playable link

The game is published as a claude.ai Artifact at https://claude.ai/artifact/QfPRRyNXi3X4tXz83RVvNV. To update it, run `npm run build:artifact` (`ARTIFACT=1` in vite.config.js) and publish `game/dist-artifact/artifact.html` to that URL with `root: "game/dist-artifact"` (relative to where you run from) and every `.png` in that folder as `files` (the list is in `dist-artifact/files.json`). That build inlines only sprites under 5 KB and keeps the bigger ones (about 210) as separate files beside the page, so the page stays about 3 MB: the 12 MB all-in-one page was too large for public sharing. `npm run build` / `npm test` still make the all-in-one `dist/index.html` that works from `file://`.
