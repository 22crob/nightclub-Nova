# Reference notes: the Nightclub City look

What we take from the owner's Nightclub City screenshots. We match the
style with our own assets and never copy its images, logo or name. Check
new floors, walls, bars, booths and decorations against this list.

## Overall feel
- Chunky, toy-like shapes with soft bevels, and glossy, saturated colours.
- Clubs are packed: the floor is crowded with furniture, people and light.
  Items read clearly at a small size because of strong colour contrast.
- Dark rooms lit by the props themselves: glowing panels, rings, tubes,
  candles and spotlights.

## Props seen, by type
- **Speakers:** tall black or grey cabinets, about 1.3x a character's
  height, one tile. A big woofer at the bottom and a smaller one above,
  each with a glowing cyan ring. Often placed in pairs beside the DJ booth.
- **Seating:** curved booth sofas in red velvet (also black leather or
  teal), wrapped around a small table with candles or drinks, usually
  2x2. Dark leather couches in rows with low tables. Tall white bar stools
  with red cushions, lined up in rows.
- **Glow pieces:** a tall lava lamp, a glowing cyan tube column,
  aquariums (glass cubes with fish and plants, lit blue).
- **Statues and fun:** a giant lucky-cat-style statue, a golden trophy
  statue, a punching bag, a dartboard, a pool table, stacks of beer
  crates.
- **Entrance:** red velvet rope between brass posts, red carpet.
- **Lights:** spotlights shining down from above onto the dance floor, a
  lighting truss over the DJ.
- **Bars:** long counters with backlit bottle shelves behind (purple or
  blue backlight), rows of beer taps, high stools in front.
- **DJ booths:** grey or black decks with glowing details, a speaker
  stack on each side.

## Floors
- Black-and-white checker, grey stone tiles, red carpet, and the classic
  light-up dance floor made of round lights (blue, cyan and yellow
  circles) as well as square ones.

## Walls
- Dark walls with glowing purple or blue panels, and backlit shelving full
  of bottles as a whole-wall finish. Brick or wood in the simpler clubs.
  Ideas to add later: a backlit bottle-shelf wall and a purple glow panel
  wall.

## Gameplay hints
- Nightclub City shows two club stats: Popularity and Luxury. Decorations
  feed Luxury. Our Vibe stat could later take a similar boost from
  decorations.
