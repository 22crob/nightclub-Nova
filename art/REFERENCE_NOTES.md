# Reference notes: the Nightclub City look

What we take from the owner's Nightclub City screenshots. We match the
style with our own assets and never copy its images, logo or name. Check
new floors, walls, bars, booths and decorations against this list.

## Overall feel
- Chunky, toy-like shapes with soft bevels, and glossy, saturated colours.
- Clubs are packed: the floor is crowded with furniture, people and light.
  Items read clearly at a small size because of strong colour contrast.
- Dark rooms lit by the props themselves: glowing panels, rings, tubes,
  candles and spotlights.

## Props seen, by type
- **Speakers:** tall black or grey cabinets, about 1.3x a character's
  height, one tile. A big woofer at the bottom and a smaller one above,
  each with a glowing cyan ring. Often placed in pairs beside the DJ booth.
- **Seating:** curved booth sofas in red velvet (also black leather or
  teal), wrapped around a small table with candles or drinks, usually
  2x2. Dark leather couches in rows with low tables. Tall white bar stools
  with red cushions, lined up in rows.
- **Glow pieces:** a tall lava lamp, a glowing cyan tube column,
  aquariums (glass cubes with fish and plants, lit blue).
- **Statues and fun:** a giant lucky-cat-style statue, a golden trophy
  statue, a punching bag, a dartboard, a pool table, stacks of beer
  crates.
- **Entrance:** red velvet rope between brass posts, red carpet.
- **Lights:** spotlights shining down from above onto the dance floor, a
  lighting truss over the DJ.
- **Bars:** long counters with backlit bottle shelves behind (purple or
  blue backlight), rows of beer taps, high stools in front.
- **DJ booths:** grey or black decks with glowing details, a speaker
  stack on each side.

## Floors
- Black-and-white checker, grey stone tiles, red carpet, and the classic
  light-up dance floor made of round lights (blue, cyan and yellow
  circles) as well as square ones.

## Walls
- Dark walls with glowing purple or blue panels, and backlit shelving full
  of bottles as a whole-wall finish. Brick or wood in the simpler clubs.
  Ideas to add later: a backlit bottle-shelf wall and a purple glow panel
  wall.

## Gameplay hints
- Nightclub City shows two club stats: Popularity and Luxury. Decorations
  feed Luxury. Our Vibe stat could later take a similar boost from
  decorations.

## Owner's screenshots, batch 2 (booths, clubs and the shop)
The owner shared these as references for assets now and later, and for the
shop UI. Take the ideas, never the images.

### Booths and seating
- **Glossy blue lounge:** two benches facing each other across a table,
  each bench backed by a row of rounded, shell-shaped cushions; round
  glowing cyan cup holders set into the table; a chrome base with a light
  strip. (Our Glow Lounge.)
- **Wood-walled booth:** a low wood wall with studs along the front, dark
  leather tufted seats along the back, small square wood tables with a
  candle each. Units line up into one long booth along a wall. (Our Wood
  Lounge.)
- **White tulip set:** white shell chairs round lime-green pedestal
  tables, in clusters. (Our Tulip Lounge.)
- **Red heart booths:** red curved two-seat chairs in pairs facing a small
  table, on a black base with a white rim.
- **White sectional booths:** white sofas round a table with gold
  bottles, behind crystal partition walls.
- **Purple-lit white cube seats** in long rows along a wall.
- Small square booths of red cushions on black low walls, placed in rows
  round the dance floor.

### Decorations and walls
- **Crystal partitions:** frosted glass panels with a diamond pattern in
  chrome frames, used to divide the room into areas (VIP corner, lounge).
- **Glow columns:** tall glass tubes lit cyan, purple or pink (lava-lamp
  pillars), dotted round the dance floor.
- **Speaker walls:** a whole wall of speaker cabinets with yellow and cyan
  cones; tall speaker stacks beside the DJ.
- **LED dot walls:** black walls covered in small white lights.
- A small indoor pool or jacuzzi with blue water, silver disco balls on
  stands, standing torches, gold wall art, potted yellow flowers.
- Red walls, brass stanchions with velvet rope, a red-cushioned bench
  row along a wall.

### Floors
- Light-up cyan and blue checker dance floors, with a pink or purple
  edge; black floors with tiny white dots; diamond-plate grey tiles.

### The shop and HUD
- **Top left:** avatar portrait with a round level badge, a blue XP bar
  beside it, then the cash with a money icon.
- **Top right:** blue boxes for Popularity and Luxury.
- **Bottom dock:** a glossy blue bar with a row of category icons on top
  (NEW, floors, DJ decks, booths, sofas, tiles, speakers, vases, staff),
  and the items below standing on the panel. Each item has its price
  under it and a "NEW" tag when just unlocked; arrows left and right page
  through.
- **Left of the dock:** round buttons for Edit Furniture, Edit Floor,
  Store, Inventory (with a count) and Clear Club.
- **Right of the dock:** a big green SAVE (or OK) button, a cash-register
  Sell button and the Luxury total.
- Zoom + and - buttons and a vertical slider on the right edge.

### In play
- Tips float up over guests in red: "+2x Tip!", "$45".
- A busy club packs guests shoulder to shoulder, with areas split up by
  partitions: bar along the back wall, dance floor in a corner, booths
  and lounges in between.
